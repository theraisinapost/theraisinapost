Source and limitations

Search uses the OpenSansad Lok Sabha Q&A dataset by Sammit Jain (CC BY 4.0), accessed through the Hugging Face Dataset Viewer API. Original government answers are linked to Digital Sansad. The automated fallback can also retrieve bounded, official question metadata directly from the Digital Sansad API, provided the source is reachable.

Keyword search covers matches returned by the upstream service; the selected Lok Sabha and question-type filters apply to the returned page, not an independently indexed full database. Empty results, rate limits and upstream failures are disclosed. A saved recent-record snapshot is used only when live browsing is unavailable and is labelled as such.
