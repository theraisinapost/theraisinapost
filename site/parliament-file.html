The document is the authority

This index presents metadata from official Parliament endpoints. A committee report is not legislation, executive approval or proof of implementation. We do not publish automatically generated summaries or allegations.

Scope & provenance

Initial automated coverage focuses on Lok Sabha–chaired departmental committees and Parliament's three financial committees. Only records with links validated against official parliamentary domains are shown. Coverage is partial where a source cannot be reached.

Built with reference to ParliamentWatch (MIT), by Pranay Kotasthane. Original reporting and documents remain with Digital Sansad and ePARLIB. The index checks official metadata daily; new reports are not independently analysed by this website.
