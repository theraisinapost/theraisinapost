Corrections and contact.

Contact the publication with a correction request, supporting documents or an editorial enquiry.

SEND A CORRECTION

Submit a correction request.

Use the editorial address below or reply to a newsletter. We review supporting material before changing an article.

raisinapost@substack.com

You can also contact us through the publication's Substack page.

What to include

  1. The article headline and its original URL.
  2. The precise sentence, number, image or claim in question.
  3. What you believe is incorrect and why.
  4. Links to original documents, datasets or credible corroborating sources, where possible.

About sensitive material. Do not send confidential records, financial credentials or another person's private information through ordinary email. No secure anonymous submission service is offered on this website.

What happens next

01

We examine the cited passage and assess the evidence supplied.

02

A material factual error is corrected in the public article, with a note describing a substantive change and its date.

03

New information or a disagreement over interpretation may call for clarification or further reporting rather than a factual correction.

We do not promise a fixed response time, automatic publication of submissions or confidentiality via regular email. Our published editorial policy is the authoritative reference.
