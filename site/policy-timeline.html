Milestones and sources

  1. 11 Aug 2023
    DPDP Act receives assent

    Act No. 22 of 2023 became law. Commencement of individual provisions was to follow by notification.

    Official document
    Published
  2. 3 Jan 2025
    Draft rules published

    MeitY issued draft rules for comment, before publication of the final notification.

    Official document
    Published
  3. 13 Nov 2025
    Final DPDP Rules notified

    G.S.R. 846(E) appeared in the Gazette. Its publication date is shown as 13 November 2025.

    Official document
    Published
  4. 13 Nov 2025
    First provisions commence

    G.S.R. 843(E) brought specified preliminary and institutional provisions into force.

    Official document
    Published
  5. 13 Nov 2026
    Second stage scheduled

    One-year commencement stage for section 6(9) and section 27(1)(d), as specified by G.S.R. 843(E).

    Official document
    Scheduled
  6. 13 May 2027
    Further provisions scheduled

    Eighteen-month stage for the remaining specified provisions, subject to subsequent changes.

    Official document
    Scheduled
