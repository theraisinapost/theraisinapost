Calculation

Equivalent amount = starting amount × end-year CPI ÷ start-year CPI. Cumulative change = (end-year CPI ÷ start-year CPI − 1) × 100. Both selected years must have positive published observations. No interpolation or forecasts are used; calculations use unrounded index values.
