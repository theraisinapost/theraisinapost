Source and limitations

Data comes from UN Comtrade public preview API under its free access and record limits. Reporter is India (code 699). The HS chapter or total aggregate and partner are preserved exactly as reported in each response.

Trade is expressed in nominal US dollars and may be revised. Mirror trade statistics from partners need not match India's own reported data. This is a lookup, not a complete downloadable historical trade database; when the external API fails, only independently retrieved matching cached observations may be displayed.
