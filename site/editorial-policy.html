Editorial policy.

The standards governing source selection, analysis, corrections and commercial disclosures at The Raisina Post.

01 / EDITORIAL PURPOSE

Coverage priorities

We follow decisions with significant consequences for India—how public institutions function, how economic choices are made and how global developments alter India's options.

Coverage priorities reflect public significance and the available evidence. Articles provide the context required to assess a development and identify the limits of the source material.

02 / SOURCES & VERIFICATION

Sources and verification

When available, we begin with original documents: legislation, court orders, regulatory notices, published datasets, company disclosures and official statements. We cross-check important assertions against credible attributed reporting and link consequential claims to relevant material.

A quotation, named source or numerical comparison should be traceable. Statistics need their reference periods and units. Different accounts of an event are not merged into a false consensus; material disputes and unresolved points should remain visible.

A useful distinction

Announced spending is not expenditure. A proposed law is not legislation in force. An allegation is not an established finding.

03 / REPORTING & ANALYSIS

Fact, interpretation and opinion

The Power Brief is a sourced editorial synthesis. Analysis examines the possible implications of established facts and is identified as analysis rather than presented as a reported development.

Guest arguments and opinion, when published, are labelled and attributed. We do not invent interviews, quotations, eyewitness accounts or access to unpublished records. Document analysis is described as document analysis; it is not disguised as field reporting.

04 / CORRECTIONS

Corrections and updates

Material factual errors are corrected in the publicly available article. A significant correction should identify what changed and when. Ordinary spelling or formatting fixes may be made without a separate note.

We assess claims of error against the evidence. Disagreement with an interpretation is not automatically a factual correction, but reasoned challenges and newly available documents deserve consideration.

05 / IMAGES, AUDIO & AI

Images, audio and production tools

Illustrations are interpretations, not photographs of documented events. AI-generated illustrations are labelled; photographs require appropriate reuse rights and credits. Synthetic voices used in audio are disclosed.

Technology may support research organisation, writing or production. It does not independently establish a fact, authenticate a source or take editorial responsibility for what appears under The Raisina Post's name.

06 / ADVERTISING & CONFLICTS

Commercial relationships and disclosures

Our published policy does not allow third-party advertising, paid placement or payment in return for favourable coverage. Relevant personal or commercial interests are disclosed rather than left for readers to discover.

A labelled Sunday house message concerning Alle's ClinX, a brand of Champaran Innovatives Private Limited, is an affiliated communication. It is separate from the selection, evidence and conclusions of editorial stories.

07 / ACCESS & CHANGES

Reader access and future revisions

The current briefings and explainers are free to read on Substack. This may evolve, but a change to access or editorial standards should not be implied retroactively.

The authoritative policy was last marked 25 October 2026 when this guide was prepared. Future material changes are to be dated on the published Substack policy page.

08 / CONTACT

Questions and evidence

Corrections, factual challenges, documents and reporting questions may be sent by replying to an edition or writing to raisinapost@substack.com. Please identify the exact passage and relevant evidence when raising an error.
