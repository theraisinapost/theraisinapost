THE PUBLICATION / READER INFORMATION

Publication guide.

A practical guide to reading, contacting and engaging with The Raisina Post. Our journalism and original editions are published on Substack.

01 / HOW WE WORK

Editorial approach

The Raisina Post focuses on governance, business, public institutions and global affairs. We aim to distinguish confirmed facts from interpretation, use identifiable sources wherever possible and make the relevance of an important development clear to the reader.

When an article draws on publicly available reports, official documents or data, readers should be able to follow the cited sources in the original publication.

02 / THE RECORD

Corrections & feedback

If you see a factual error, missing context or a broken citation, please contact the publication through its Substack page. Include the article title, the relevant passage and supporting evidence. We aim to review substantive feedback and clarify or correct mistakes where warranted.

03 / WRITE TO THE DESK

Submissions & correspondence

For story ideas, guest analysis, letters or information relevant to our coverage, start with the publication’s About and contact page. Please identify what can be independently verified and whether the information is intended for publication. Do not send sensitive personal information through unsecured channels.

04 / REUSE

Rights & attribution

Headlines and short excerpts appear in this website’s RSS-based index, with links back to the original editions on Substack. The presence of an item in this index does not grant permission to reproduce full articles, images or other copyrighted material. Contact the publication before seeking republication rights.

05 / THE INDEX

About this archive

This lightweight website lists recent public items available from the official Substack RSS feed. The search field looks through items in that feed, not through the entire publication history. For older editions and original full-length articles, visit the Substack archive.
