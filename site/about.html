Our purpose

An announcement is the beginning of an inquiry, not its conclusion.

Important developments rarely arrive as complete stories. A regulatory decision may reshape an industry long after the circular is issued. A company's ownership may explain the incentives behind its strategy. A headline GDP number can conceal the effects of revisions and methodology.

Our work is to stay with those questions. We examine primary records, compare claims with available evidence, explain institutions in context and identify the gaps that honest reporting cannot yet close. The result is journalism for people who need to understand not only what happened, but what it changes.

How we work

Every piece should help the reader distinguish the evidence from the interpretation.

01

Begin with the record

We prioritise court orders, regulatory filings, parliamentary material, published datasets, official notices and named institutional research. We also attribute credible external reporting.

02

Establish what is known

A proposal is not a law in force; a policy announcement is not evidence of implementation. Where sources disagree or data is incomplete, the uncertainty belongs in the story.

03

Separate reporting from analysis

Consequences, incentives and scenarios may be assessed, but analysis is presented as analysis. We do not imply interviews, access or firsthand observation that did not occur.

04

Remain accountable

Material errors warrant correction. Sources, editorial limitations and relevant relationships should be visible so readers can evaluate the work on its merits.

Editorial independence

Credibility depends on making the boundaries visible.

Our published editorial policy rejects paid placement and payment for favourable reporting. Relevant affiliations are disclosed rather than hidden. This includes an affiliated house message for Alle's ClinX, a brand of Champaran Innovatives Private Limited; such a message is not an editorial source or instruction.

Readers should be able to inspect the distinction between our reporting, outside material and any commercial relationship. Our Publication Guide explains sourcing, corrections, disclosures and the limits of an RSS-powered index.
