About the publicationIndependent journalism · India & the world

The record matters. So does what follows.

The Raisina Post examines decisions that shape India. We follow institutions, read the underlying evidence and explain consequences that do not always fit inside a headline.

Our work is for readers who value a considered account of public decisions over the speed of a news cycle.

01

Our purpose

The announcement is the beginning of the story, not the end.

Policy takes shape inside ministries and legislatures, but its consequences appear in budgets, institutions, boardrooms and daily life. A cabinet approval is not an implemented programme. A target is not an outcome. A public claim is not established simply because it was repeated.

We work from primary documents, official statistics, corporate filings and attributed reporting wherever possible. We explain the limits of what those materials establish, including where evidence is incomplete or opinions reasonably differ.

03

How we work

The most useful reporting explains not only what is said, but what the record can support.

01

Begin with the evidence

Source important claims, link consequential documents and distinguish direct evidence from another party's interpretation.

02

Distinguish decisions from results

An announced plan, approved policy, statutory requirement and measured outcome each mean something different. We make those distinctions explicit.

03

Keep uncertainty in view

Figures need dates, units and context. Where accounts conflict or data are revised, the reporting should make the limitation visible.

04

Accept correction

Material factual errors are corrected in public, with a note describing significant changes. New evidence can change a conclusion.

Read the editorial standards

Independence

We make commercial relationships visible.

Under our published editorial policy, The Raisina Post does not accept third-party advertising, paid placement or payment for favourable coverage. Relevant personal and business conflicts are disclosed.
