About the publicationPolicy, business & the economy

The Raisina Post. Policy and business in context.

The Raisina Post is a publication covering Indian public policy, business and the economy. Our briefings and analysis examine significant decisions and their implications for institutions and industry.

For professionals and readers who need a clear view of developments affecting India.

01

Our focus

Understand the decision. Assess its implications.

We examine how policy and economic decisions develop, what they change and how implementation progresses. Coverage connects official announcements with the legal, financial and institutional context needed to assess them.

Our work draws on primary documents, official statistics, corporate filings and attributed reporting. Sources and reference periods accompany material claims; analysis identifies assumptions and gaps in the available evidence.

03

How we work

Our editorial process is organised around source quality, accurate interpretation and clear attribution.

01

Source selection

Use original documents where available, attribute material claims and provide links readers can consult.

02

Context and status

Identify the status of proposals, approvals and statutory requirements, and distinguish them from measured outcomes.

03

Data and limitations

Include dates, units and relevant comparisons. Identify revisions, conflicting accounts and limitations that affect interpretation.

04

Review and corrections

Correct material factual errors in the published article and record significant changes. Review conclusions when new evidence becomes available.
