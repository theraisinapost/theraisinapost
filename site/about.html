ABOUT THE PUBLICATION

India's decisions, made clearer.

The Raisina Post is an independent publication following the decisions that shape India: who makes them, what the record shows, and what their consequences may be. We write for readers who want more than the announcement.

Why we exist

Important decisions seldom fit neatly into a single headline. A cabinet announcement may need months of implementation; a quarterly statistic can hide a change in methodology; a court order may settle a legal question without settling the underlying policy debate. We believe useful journalism holds those distinctions in view.

Our starting point is usually a document: legislation, judgments, public data, budget statements, company records or institutional disclosures. We put that material in context, assess what can be established and identify the next evidence that would change the account. We also use attributed reporting from credible news organisations when direct materials do not tell the whole story.

Our coverage

The Editorial Desk concentrates on public policy, governance, institutions and accountability; the economy, financial markets and business; technology and infrastructure; and international developments with consequences for India. These areas overlap. The interesting question is often not which section a story belongs to, but why a decision in one place changes outcomes somewhere else.

How we work

We identify facts as facts, projections as projections and analysis as interpretation. We distinguish a proposed policy from one in force, a commitment from a completed project, and an allegation from a verified finding. We attribute sources, explain material limitations and do not imply interviews or on-the-ground reporting when a piece is based on document analysis.

Readers can see our detailed editorial approach and the original editorial policy on Substack. We correct substantive errors publicly and disclose relevant commercial or personal interests.

A note on independence

Editorial independence includes making relationships visible. The publication's policy does not accept third-party advertising, paid placement or payment for favourable reporting. It also discloses an affiliated Sunday house message for Alle's ClinX, a brand of Champaran Innovatives Private Limited. Such messages are separate from editorial judgments. Readers can examine the full disclosure in the published policy.
