# Personal desk and membership preview

The public `desk.html` brings existing source-backed research into one reading flow. `membership.html` explains the planned paid service and routes readers to the existing free Substack signup. No new subscriber database or payment provider is configured.

## Publishing and maintenance

- Briefing previews come from the existing validated `posts.json` feed, with original dates and links. The desk does not claim to publish a new daily edition when the feed has not changed.
- Focus filtering uses the publication's existing section classification. Policy covers Governance and The Power Brief; Business covers Business; Economy includes Business and general Reporting. These are broad reading preferences, not individual article topic tags.
- Visit comparison counts URLs added to the current feed, not material changes within an article. Preferences, reading density and visit history remain in this browser under `raisina-desk-v1`; reset removes them.
- Dossiers link the maintained data-protection timeline, annual economic indicators and TCS ownership atlas. Review their underlying records rather than presenting old data as live.
- The policy-to-business example separates sourced chronology from editorial questions. Expand the template with verified changes, affected organisations, source passages, unresolved questions and the next dated milestone when preparing future briefings.
- The release calendar imports the recorded MoSPI schedule. Downloaded ICS files are snapshots; readers must recheck the source for amendments. Stable event IDs preserve identity when an event moves out of the upcoming set. No alarm or email notification is created.
- Print/save as PDF uses the browser print dialog and a dedicated stylesheet. CSV exports remain available through the existing finance charts.

## Before paid launch

Select an authentication, billing and private-content backend; establish prices and cancellation/access terms; connect webhook-verified entitlements; enforce access on the server and keep paid material out of the public Pages bundle. Browser-local preferences are not membership credentials. Migrate or associate preferences only with reader consent.

Substack signup remains a free newsletter subscription, not a premium waiting-list confirmation. Campaign parameters identify the entry route but do not by themselves create subscriber tags or prove attribution. No paid checkout, secure paywall, cross-device account or automated editorial service is claimed by this release.
